# Metaphysics Monday: Are You Still You?
## Slide Outline — High School Philosophy

### Slide 1 — Title
- Metaphysics Monday: Are You Still You?
- Personal Identity & the Puzzle of Change

### Slide 2 — Warm-Up Prompt
- List five things that make you *you*:
  - A scar
  - A memory
  - A habit
  - A relationship
  - A belief

### Slide 3 — The Surgeon's Question
- If each item on your list were replaced, one at a time...
- At what point did you stop being you?
- (Most answers: "somewhere in the middle" — but there's no clean line)

### Slide 4 — The Gradual Swap
- Replace one cell per day
- By year seven: zero original cells remain
- Did you die and get replaced by a copy?
- Or did you just continue?

### Slide 5 — Same Stuff vs. Same Story
- Identity might not live in the parts
- It might live in the *pattern* — the ongoing narrative
- Ship of Theseus: replace every plank, is it still the ship?

### Slide 6 — The Hard One
- A machine scans your brain perfectly
- Builds an identical copy: your memories, your plans
- The original you is still standing there
- Which one gets your life?

### Slide 7 — The Loud Question
- Your house. Your job. Your relationships.
- Who inherits them — and why?

### Slide 8 — Closing Thought
- Maybe a person isn't a thing
- Maybe it's an event
- A wave isn't the water — it's what the water is doing

### Slide 9 — Optional: Go Deeper
- Parfit's teleporter: step in, step out on Mars — travel or death?
- Fission: one person splits into two copies — which is original?
- Neuroscience hook: what the brain actually says about identity

### Slide 10 — Norms
- No wrong answers, only undefended ones
- Say *why* before you say *what*
